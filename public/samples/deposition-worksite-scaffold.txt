RECORD OF EXAMINATION OF THE SECOND DEFENDANT'S SITE SUPERVISOR
Taken on 2 April 2026 at 9.30 am

Present:  Ms Priya Balakrishnan (Counsel for the Plaintiff)
          Mr Daniel Wong (Counsel for the First Defendant)
          Mr Firdaus Salleh (Counsel for the Second Defendant)

---

Q1.  Please state your name and your role on the Jurong Gateway project.
A.   Ang Chee Keong. I am the site supervisor employed by Ascent Formwork and
     Scaffold. I have been in scaffolding for nineteen years.

Q2.  Who engaged Ascent for this project?
A.   Keppel Ridge is the main contractor. Ascent is a subcontractor. We were
     engaged under a works agreement signed in January 2025.

Q3.  Does the main contractor direct your men day to day?
A.   Not directly. They tell us which zone and by when. How we erect the
     scaffold is our method. Their safety officer does walk the site and he can
     stop our work if he sees something wrong.

Q4.  On 3 September 2025, what work was being carried out at block C?
A.   We were striking the scaffold on the east elevation, from level four down.

Q5.  Was a permit to work issued that morning?
A.   Yes. A dismantling permit was issued at 8.15 am.

Q6.  Was a risk assessment done for that activity?
A.   There is a risk assessment in the safety manual for scaffold dismantling.
     It was done at the start of the project, not that morning.

Q7.  Did the crew receive a toolbox briefing that day?
A.   Normally yes. That morning I was called to the site office at 8.00 am, so
     the briefing was taken by my leading hand. I did not see the attendance
     sheet afterwards.

Q8.  Mr Rizwan was working on the platform at level three. Was he trained for
     dismantling work?
A.   He had the general safety induction. He did not have the scaffold erector
     certificate. He was supposed to be passing materials only, not on the
     platform.

Q9.  Who instructed him to go onto the platform?
A.   I cannot say for certain. I was not there at that moment.

Q10. Was he wearing a harness?
A.   He had a harness but the lanyard was not anchored. That is what I was told
     afterwards by the men.

Q11. What caused the platform to give way?
A.   Two transoms had been removed out of sequence from the level below. The
     boards above lost their support.

Q12. Is removing transoms out of sequence a departure from the method statement?
A.   Yes. The method statement requires top down, one lift at a time.

Q13. Was there any supervision on the platform at the time?
A.   No one from Ascent was on that lift at that moment.

Q14. Did the main contractor's safety officer inspect the scaffold that week?
A.   He inspects weekly. I believe the last inspection was on 29 August. I
     don't recall seeing the inspection record for the week of the incident.

Q15. Was there a photograph or log of the scaffold condition after the fall?
A.   The Ministry of Manpower officer took photographs. We also have the daily
     log book but I have not reviewed it since.

Q16. What injuries did Mr Rizwan sustain?
A.   He fell about four metres. Fractured pelvis and a head injury. He was in
     hospital for three weeks and he has not returned to work.

Q17. Since the incident, has anything changed on site?
A.   Yes. Now every dismantling activity needs a fresh toolbox briefing with a
     signed attendance sheet, and only certified erectors go on the lift.
