Suit No 412 of 2025

Between
    LIM WEI SHAN                                      ... Plaintiff

    HARBOURPOINT RETAIL MANAGEMENT PTE LTD             ... Defendant

RECORD OF EXAMINATION OF THE PLAINTIFF
Taken on 14 March 2026 at 10.05 am

Present:  Mr Rajan Menon (Counsel for the Plaintiff)
          Ms Chloe Tan (Counsel for the Defendant)

---

Q1.  Ms Lim, please state your full name and occupation for the record.
A.   Lim Wei Shan. I am a dental hygienist. I work at a clinic in Toa Payoh.

Q2.  I want to take you to 8 November 2025. Where were you at about 7.40 pm?
A.   I was at Harbourpoint Mall, level two, walking from the escalator towards
     the supermarket entrance.

Q3.  Describe the floor at that location.
A.   It is the polished stone floor they have throughout that level. That
     evening there was a puddle near the entrance, quite a large one. It looked
     like water had been tracked in from outside because it was raining.

Q4.  Was there any warning sign?
A.   No. There was no warning sign and no barricade. I have thought about this a
     lot. If there had been a cone I would have gone around it.

Q5.  What happened next?
A.   My right foot slipped from under me. I fell backwards and landed on my left
     wrist and hip. I could not get up on my own.

Q6.  Did anyone from the mall come to assist you?
A.   A cleaner came after maybe three or four minutes. She was pushing a mop
     bucket. She said something like "aiyah, this floor always like that when
     rain". Then a supervisor came with an incident report form.

Q7.  Was the supervisor a mall employee?
A.   I am not sure. His shirt said Sparkle Facilities. I think the cleaning is
     outsourced to a contractor, but I cannot confirm that.

Q8.  Do you know how long the puddle had been there before you fell?
A.   I don't recall exactly. I did not see it before I stepped in it. But the
     cleaner's comment made me think it had been there a while.

Q9.  Was there CCTV in that area?
A.   Yes, there is a camera above the supermarket entrance. I asked for the
     footage in my letter of demand. I have not seen any recording.

Q10. What footwear were you wearing?
A.   Flat leather sandals. Not heels.

Q11. Were you carrying anything or in a hurry?
A.   I had one shopping bag. I was walking normally. I was not rushing.

Q12. Tell me about your injuries.
A.   A distal radius fracture in my left wrist. I had surgery on 10 November and
     a plate was inserted. I was on medical leave for eleven weeks and I still
     do physiotherapy twice a month.

Q13. Had you injured that wrist before?
A.   No. Never. I had a pre-existing lower back condition from 2019 but nothing
     with the wrist.

Q14. How has this affected your work?
A.   I cannot hold the scaler steadily for a full session. My clinic reduced my
     hours. My income dropped by about a third.

Q15. Are you aware of any inspection records for that floor?
A.   My solicitors asked for the cleaning log book and the inspection schedule.
     I understand nothing has been produced yet.
